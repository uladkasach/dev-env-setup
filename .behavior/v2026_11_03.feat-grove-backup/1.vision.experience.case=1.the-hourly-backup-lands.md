# case=1 — the hourly backup lands, and nobody lifted a finger

critipath · happy · cells b6, b7, b8, b10, b12, o1 · sketch, not proof

## .narrative

**the clone's afternoon.** a clone on `grove-1` works in `_worktrees/svc-quotes.feat-x`. it commits
twice and pushes neither. its conversation grows line by line in its session file. at 15:00 the
agent reads its whole tree; the clone never knows. no prompt lands on its pane, its session file
keeps its mtime, its next append lands as normal. the box still dozes off on time when the clone
goes quiet.

**the commander's glance.** at 15:20 he runs the read he runs at the start of each day,
`rhx git.grove.stats`, and one row he never asked for says `backup: ✓ 20m ago`. he does not think
about it again. that is the point: the two unpushed commits and the conversation that wrote them
now live in two places.
