## .the restore proof, reshaped (S3)

the full restore onto a fresh grove is already proven, so no fresh grove is owed. the live proof is a
partial restore laid aside on the backed-up grove itself, then compared to the live tree:

- `rhx grove.backup.restore --from <grove> --of <org/repo> --tree <dir> --into <dir>`: the tree
  lands as a clone of its own, from origin and its unit alone, and its clone conversation matches
  the live one

  plaintext credential
