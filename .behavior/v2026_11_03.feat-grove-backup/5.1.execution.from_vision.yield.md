## .the scope, narrowed to camper (S4)

the agent backs up camper's `~/git`, `~/.claude`, `~/.rhachet` and no other seat: only camper holds
work, the scope `git backup` carries. it runs as camper (`User=camper`), so it reaches aws through
camper's own config on ec2 and on hetzner, and owes no `/etc/grove/aws.env`. vision inventory b14
("every seat") is superseded. the key keeps `user=<seat>`.

## .the restore proof, reshaped (S3)

the full restore onto a fresh grove is already proven, so no fresh grove is owed. the live proof is a
partial restore laid aside on the backed-up grove itself, then compared to the live tree:

- `rhx grove.backup.restore --from <grove> --of <org/repo> --tree <dir> --into <dir>`: the tree
  lands as a clone of its own, from origin and its unit alone, and its clone conversation matches
  the live one
- `rhx grove.backup.restore --from <grove> --home .claude --into <dir>`: the home dir lands, with no
  plaintext credential

⚠️ a clone's conversation lives INSIDE its tree, at `.agent/.actors/<actor>/brain/.claude/projects`,
so it rides in the tree's unit. a grove's `~/.claude/projects` may hold none.

`prove.grove-backup-live` runs both, as camper: `rhx git.grove.send <grove> --reply --play prove.grove-backup-live`.

## .one widen of the vision, noted

b10's rule reads "every changed path is a `projects/**/*.jsonl`". the agent accepts any `*.jsonl` or
dir inside `~/.claude`: a live brain also appends to `~/.claude/history.jsonl` and adds entries to its
dirs, and to fail `.claude` on those would cry wolf on every busy grove. any other changed file still
takes b9's retry.
