# case=1 — the backup lands, one tree at a time, and nobody lifted a finger

critipath · happy · cells b6, b7, b8, b10, b21, b26, o1 · sketch, not proof

## .narrative

**the clone's afternoon.** a clone on `grove-1` works in `_worktrees/svc-quotes.feat-x`. it commits
twice and pushes neither. its conversation grows line by line in its session file, inside the tree
at `.agent/.actors/<actor>/brain/.claude/projects/`. on the quarter hour the agent reads the tree's
metadata, sees it moved, and packs one object: the two commits origin lacks, the files that differ
from HEAD, and the conversation. the clone never knows. no prompt lands on its pane, its session
file keeps its mtime, its next append lands as normal.

the other forty trees on the box did not move. each costs a metadata read; none is packed.

**the box goes down mid-pass.** a hetzner reboot cuts a pass short after twelve trees. each of those
twelve keeps its mark. the next pass starts at the stalest tree, the first the cut pass never
reached, and the box is whole again within one interval.

**the commander's glance.** he runs the read he runs at the start of each day,
`rhx git.grove.stats`, and one row he never asked for says `backup: ✓ 4m ago`. the two unpushed
commits, and the conversation that wrote them, now live in two places — and the tree's unit alone
restores both.
