## .read — 9 cells

the consumer: a claude session loads home and runs its hooks. the write bit and the patch do not
change what a read runs, so the cells collapse on `role hooks`. what a home role hook does to a
session depends on the session kind, so each row splits on it: **bare** (no roles enrolled in its
dir) or **enrolled** (claude started with `--settings` from a rhachet repo — it loads home AND the
repo's own file).

| state × session | outcome | verdict | feel × care | case |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `H` × bare | foreign role hooks run on the commander — route.drive prints, check-permissions blocks `ls` | forbidden — the strip bars it, and verify holds the invariant. case 8 [c8.a] shows the before it removes | sharp × criti | case 8 [c8.a → c8.b] |
| `H` × enrolled | each role hook the repo also declares runs twice; one the repo does not declare runs anyway | forbidden — same invariant | sharp × criti | case 8 |
| `h` × bare | no role hook runs; the commander's own keys apply | demoed | happy × criti | case 8 [c8.b t0] |
| `h` × enrolled | the repo's role hooks run once, from `settings.of.repo.json` | demoed | happy × criti | case 8 [c8.b t1] |
| absent × either | claude runs with its defaults, plus `--settings` if enrolled | itemized | happy × alter | — |
