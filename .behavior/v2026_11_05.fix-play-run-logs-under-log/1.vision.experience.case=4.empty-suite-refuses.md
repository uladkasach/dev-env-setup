# case 4 — an empty suite refuses, and leaves no green to read

## narrative

the mechanic moves plays between dirs and, mid-move, runs `rhx play.run --all` on a tree whose
`.play/permanent/` holds no tracked play. before the change they would have seen
`0 passed · 0 declined · 0 failed` and `🌲 every play passed`, and the director would have lit a
green cell for a suite that ran naught. after the change they see, at once and before any play
starts: `✋ ConstraintError: no tracked play under .play/permanent`, with the fix
(`git -C <tree> ls-files .play/permanent` to read the index). their prompt shows exit 2. the
director, later, reads the prior log or `unread` — never a false green.

if the mechanic had moved the whole dir away, they meet the extant peer halt instead
(`no tracked play dir at …`, exit 2, `s0`) — the same contract: fast, loud, names the fix.
