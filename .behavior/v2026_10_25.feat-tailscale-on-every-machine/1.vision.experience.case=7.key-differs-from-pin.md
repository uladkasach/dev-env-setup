# case 7 — the commander meets a key that differs from the pin (sharp, fails safe)

## .narrative

the commander runs the bundle on a fresh laptop the week tailscale rotates its release key. he
expects the install. instead the run stops at the key with a ✋: the bytes tailscale served do not
match the pinned fingerprint, both fingerprint sets printed side by side, and the line that says
no file was written to his apt trust store. he checks the box: no tailscale, no new source. the
fix-text tells him where the pin came from and to confirm a real rotation from two channels before
the pin moves. he does not get a half-trusted box; he gets a clear stop and a next step.

## .timeline

```
given a laptop with no tailscale, the commander at his terminal with sudo warmed
  when [t0] he runs `sudo -v && rhx grove.provision --what 5.23.tailscale --mode apply`
   and the key tailscale serves differs from the pin
    then he sees provision.upsert ✋ the key does not match its pin                          # B2
     and he sees both fingerprint sets, the pinned and the served
     and he reads that no file landed in /usr/share/keyrings or /etc/apt/sources.list.d
     and he sees provision.verify ✋ tailscale absent
     and the fix-text names the pin's source, and asks for two channels before the pin moves
     and configure.upsert makes no `up` call, and configure.verify ✋ not joined — tailscale absent  # (C2)
  when [t1] he reruns, with no pin change
    then he sees the same ✋ — the refusal holds until a human moves the pin
```

## .fail-safe

fast (the check runs before any write), loud (one ✋ carries both fingerprint sets; the verify ✋s confirm tailscale absent), teaches the
fix (where the pin lives and how to verify a rotation). he can see for himself that his trust store
is untouched.
