# 1.vision.experience.dimensions

the experience: a human selects lines of what a terminal window shows, by keyboard alone, and copies them.

## .the core axes — walked as a full product

| # | dimension | values |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | **holder** — who owns the scrollback | `tmux.local` (a duct on this laptop) · `tmux.remote` (a duct on a grove, over ssh) · `kitty.main` (no tmux, a shell on kitty's main screen) · `kitty.alt` (no tmux, an app such as nvim or less on the alternate screen) |
| D2 | **intent** | `copy` (select, then copy) · `look` (enter, move, leave, no copy) · `recopy` (copy, adjust, copy again) |

D1 × D2 = 12 cells, each verdicted one by one in `1.vision.experience.case=_.md`.

## .the in-view axes — walked against each key, not per cell

| # | dimension | values |
|---|---|---|
| D3 | **view key** — every key the view binds | `right-alt+j/k` · `enter` · `ctrl+c` · `super+v` · `ctrl+x` · `q` |
| D7 | **selection state** | `none` · `active` |

D7 is what branches the keys: right-alt+j/k move the cursor (`none`) or grow and shrink the selection (`active`); enter begins (`none`) or ends (`active`); ctrl+c leaves (`none`) or copies and stays (`active`); super+v, ctrl+x, and q leave either way and drop an `active` selection uncopied. both holders bind the same branches, so D3 × D7 is walked once as the key table in case=4, not per D1 × D2 cell.

## .the content axis — void by nature

the holder's content (full, short, or empty scrollback) yields no distinct experience, so it carries no axis: right-alt+k clamps at the oldest line in both holders (tmux's cursor stops at the top of its history; nvim's at line 1), and a selection over blank lines copies blank lines. the gesture behaves the same on any amount of text.

## .the sub-type axes — attributes of a branch of D1, not orthogonal to it

| # | dimension | applies to | values |
|---|---|---|---|
| D4 | **far conf** | `tmux.remote` only — only a grove's tmux.conf can differ from this checkout | `converged` · `stale` |
| D5 | **terminal** | the `tmux.*` holders only — the overlay is a kitty window, so no other terminal can hold `kitty.*` | `kitty` · `other` |
| D6 | **copy-mode entry** | the `tmux.*` holders only — the overlay opens one way | `super+v` · `the old way` (mouse wheel, ctrl+right-alt+k) |

each is void outside its branch for the reason stated in its row, so no column of the product hides a cell. each value is walked in the inventory's sub-type rows.

## .the hops a copy crosses — walked as a sharp variant of each copy cell

a copy is worth naught until it reaches the desktop clipboard. so each `copy` cell has a sharp twin: the copy lands in the holder but not on the desktop. the inventory gives each holder's hop chain its own row.
