## .what the gesture must never meet

| cell | verdict | why |
|---|---|---|
| any × super+v claimed by the desktop | **forbidden** | super+v must reach kitty. today no shortcut claims it: COSMIC's shortcut defaults bind no super+v, and the custom shortcuts this repo declares (3.3.desktop) bind none either; the wish itself reports kitty receives CSI 118;9u. a future desktop bind on super+v would kill the gesture, so it is barred |
| any × select by mouse | **forbidden** | kitty owns mouse selection (`kitty.conf`, mouse_map); the selector must not claim a mouse path, or two selections would fight over one drag |
