# case 3 — a duct at work holds the box

critipath · sharp — a crew can work for hours with no new trace.

## .narrative

a clone kicks off a 4h backfill inside its duct and waits on the tool call. its brain appends no turn
until the call returns, so its trace ages past 3h. the pane prints progress every minute, so its
window's `#{window_activity}` stays fresh — with no client attached and no key pressed (A6). the
tick reads every tmux server on the box as root, takes each window's activity, and holds.
