# case 1 — a crew at work holds the box

critipath · sharp — the measured defect.

## .narrative

a clone is four stones into a route in its worktree on 0901, one of twelve crews at work. the
commander last typed five hours ago. the clone finishes a turn, starts the next, and gets an answer
back — on a box that never stopped under it. it never learns there was a question; that is the point.

today, the same clone on the same box gets no answer. its turn is cut mid-route by the box's own
`StopInstances`, along with every other crew, because the box saw no work at all: the clone's turns
are written to its worktree, at
`~/git/ehmpathy/_worktrees/rhachet.beav.<branch>/.agent/.actors/actor.via.hash=<h>/brain/.claude/projects/<dir>/<id>.jsonl`,
and today's check reads only `/home/*/.claude/projects`.

**the behavior under it.** with the fix, the 5-minute tick reads each worktree's trace dir, finds the
clone's jsonl 40s old, holds, and names that trace in the journal.
