× G2 extras: a foreign drop-in systemd reads is retired by the apply like the stopgap; a file in a

× G3: a stopped, disabled, or masked timer is armed by every ground apply (F3); an active one is
left active; a unit not loaded, or one with a reload owed, is loaded by the apply's
`daemon-reload`. the verify names each with its own `✋` → itemized (row 45). no cell changes
verdict on G3, so it is walked by that rule.

× M: every cell above is the `apply` value. under `plan` (the default), every upsert short-circuits
and every verify runs, so each cell's plan verdict is its verify read with no write: drift named on
the old-check, infra-born, absent, and stopgap cells; ✔ on the converged cell → itemized (row 22).

cell by one rule each, and none writes past its fault:
