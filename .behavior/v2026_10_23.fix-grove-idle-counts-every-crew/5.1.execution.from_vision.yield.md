## .what changed from the vision

- **an unread source holds the box, and exits 1 (F6).** vision case 7 chose sleep plus a loud line;
  5.1 holds instead. a wrong sleep kills every crew on the box; a wrong hold bills until the next
  plan names the `Result`
- **a keystroke holds a pts; output alone does not (F13).** the old check read mtime, so any
  redrawn status line held an idle box forever. a bare ssh shell that only prints, with no duct and
  no trace, now sleeps at 3h — a wisher call
- **the sleep line names its newest stir and its counts**, in the hold line's shape. the vision's
  contract table and cases 2 and 6 now carry it
- **the slug is `1.15.hibernate`**, not `1.12`: `origin/main` has taken `1.12`–`1.14` (F5)
- **the check has no knobs.** the clamp rewrites the check's absolute paths and stubs `aws`, `curl`,
  and `pgrep` on PATH, so it reaches the old bytes too — the red proof needs exactly that
- **the service is no longer byte-identical to the live one**: `TimeoutStartSec=180`
