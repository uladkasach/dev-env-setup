# case=12 — a box that already declares another platform

feel: sharp · care: critipath

## .narrative

the registry says `hetzner.hetzbot`, and the box's `/etc/grove/platform` names another platform — a
hand edit, a box rebuilt from another image, or a registry entry aimed at the wrong host. the boot
reaches step 1 and asks the ground seat to findsert the declaration. the seat finds a different
line already there, answers `DIFFERS`, and writes no byte. the boot halts at step 1: it relays the
seat's answer (any escape sequence in it stripped whole), names the read a human owes before any
apply, and hands the resume line with its verb. it never lays its own value over the box's — a
wrong declaration would point every aws road on the box at the wrong identity.

## .timeline

```
given a registry entry for <grove> with platform hetzner.hetzbot
  and the box's /etc/grove/platform names another platform
when [t0] rhx git.grove.provision boot <grove> --mode apply
  then step 1 halts: `✋ step 1 (reach) does not hold`, rc 3
  then the why relays the seat: `DIFFERS: the box declares <other>`, with no escape debris
  then the fix names the read first: `ssh <grove>.ground cat /etc/grove/platform`
  then the resume names the verb and the step: `git.grove.provision boot <grove> --mode apply --from 1 --trust keep`
  then /etc/grove/platform is unchanged, and no seat converged
when [t1] the human corrects whichever side is wrong (the registry via `git.grove.set --platform`, or the box's file)
  and reruns the resume line
  then step 1 reads `✔` with the seat's verdict, and the boot reads as case=1
```

escape) and arm 6 (the sink that strips it).
