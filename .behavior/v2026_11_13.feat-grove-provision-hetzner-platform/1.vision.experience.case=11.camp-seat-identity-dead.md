# case=11 — a camp seat whose ambient identity does not answer

feel: sharp · care: critipath

## .narrative

on a laptop, an absent ambient aws identity is normal: the camp-only bundles decline with a 🌙, and the decline is correct. on a camp seat the same silence is a fault. infra's signer unit may be stopped, the roles-anywhere session may have lapsed, or `5.6.aws` may never have converged. every reader of that identity once shared the laptop's decline. so a camp-seat apply printed success and laid no hop profile and no `AWS_PROFILE` row, and the first integration suite died hours later with no line that named the cause.

now one function gives the verdict for every reader: `5.13.reach`'s upsert and verify, and `5.12.rack`'s profile upsert and verify. on a laptop it declines with a 🌙 (rc 0). on a camp seat it halts with a ✋ (rc 1) that names `5.6.aws` as the bundle to read. on hetzner it also names a stopped signer unit and a lapsed session, and an ec2 seat names neither.

## .timeline

```
given a camp seat (cloud@hetzner.hetzbot or cloud@aws.ec2), the camper
  and `aws configure export-credentials --profile ambient` answers no credentials
when [t0] rhx grove.provision --what 5.13.reach --mode apply
  then ✋ `no ambient identity answers on this camp seat` (rc 1)
  then it names `rhx grove.provision --what 5.6.aws --mode plan`
  then on hetzner it names a stopped signer unit and a lapsed session; on ec2, no signer
  then it never says "on a laptop this is correct"
when [t1] rhx grove.provision --what 5.12.rack --mode plan
  then the profile row's verify gives the same ✋, never `declined — no ambient identity here`
given a local@unix laptop with no ambient identity
when [t2] rhx grove.provision --what 5.13.reach --mode apply
  then 🌙 declined — no ambient identity (rc 0)
```

clamp: `prove.carried-tree-serves-reach` arm 2e (three tiers, plus the three other readers on laptop and camp seat).
