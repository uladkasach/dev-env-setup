this repo has no npm test suites: its tests are the plays under `.play/permanent/`,
driven by `rhx play.run`, plus `shell.syntax.verify` and `dox.verify`. a play whose
subject needs a seat this laptop is not (passwordless sudo, earlyoom) runs on the
hetzner grove's ground seat via `rhx git.grove.send <seat> --reply --play <name>`.
