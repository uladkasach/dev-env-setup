# 1.vision.experience.dimensions — `house://` on this box

the space a collocated house grove's experiences vary over. four axes, each a small closed
set; the product is walked in `1.vision.experience.case=_.md`.

## .the axes

| # | axis | values | n |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | **actor** | `human` (the box owner, at a terminal) · `supervisor` (an agent on the visible seat, no tty) · `camper` (a crew process inside the house grove) · `ci` (a runner, no human, no prior state) | 4 |
| G | **goal** | `mint` (create the two seats) · `converge` (`grove.provision` from each seat) · `reach` (ssh / duct over loopback) · `badge` (obtain the AWS identity) · `prove` (`prove.grove-aws-identity-chain` + the ready ladder) · `sustain` (still works hours / days later, after reboot) · `del` (tear the grove down) | 7 |
| S | **box state** | `fresh` (no house grove yet) · `converged` (a healthy house grove) · `drifted` (one fact off: a key gone, a perm widened, a cert expired, sshd stopped) | 3 |
| B | **boundary probe** | `none` (the actor stays inside its own seat) · `cross` (the actor reaches for a fact that belongs to another seat — another grove's badge, the visible seat's rack, root) | 2 |

product: 4 × 7 × 3 × 2 = **168 cells**. most collapse by nature (below); the inventory
verdicts the survivors one row per distinct experience, and records each collapse with its
reason.

## .why each axis is orthogonal

- **actor ⟂ goal** — every actor can attempt every goal; whether it SHOULD is a verdict
  (forbidden), never implied by the axis. a camper can type the mint command; the behavior
  must refuse it
- **goal ⟂ box state** — every goal meets every state: a `mint` on a converged box is the
  idempotency case; a `badge` on a drifted box is the expired-cert case
- **boundary ⟂ the rest** — the `cross` value is what surfaces the sandbox claim the wish
  rests on (an AWS badge walled off from the box's other users, though never from root — which
  an EC2 grove cannot hold). it is a separate axis
  because one actor + goal + state carries a happy `none` cell and a sharp `cross` cell, and
  a happy-path list keeps only the first

## .the collapses — whole slices that cannot occur, and why

| slice | verdict | why |
|---|---|---|
| `ci × sustain` | impossible | a ci runner lives and dies within one job; there is no "days later" to sustain over |
| `ci × * × drifted` | itemized only as fixtures | a runner holds no prior state; drift exists there only where a clamp plants it on purpose |
| `supervisor × mint` / `supervisor × del` | forbidden | both need root on the host; the supervisor has no tty and must never be handed one (`rule.forbid.tty-as-a-proxy-for-a-human`) |
| `camper × mint` / `camper × del` / `camper × converge(system half)` | forbidden | a camper holds no sudo, by design |
| `human × * × cross` | impossible as an attack | the human owns the host; a cross reach by the human is administration, not a breach |

## .axes weighed and folded in, not added

| candidate | why it adds no axis | where its cells sit |
|---|---|---|
| **concurrency** — first · concurrent · retried | `retried` is `box state = converged` (a re-run meets a grove already there). `concurrent` is one new experience, not a column | retried: rows 2, 3. concurrent: row 46 |
| **actor = system** — the renewal timer, a reboot, a sleep | none chooses a goal; each is an EVENT inside `goal = sustain`, met by the camper or the supervisor | rows 36, 37, 39 |

## .the latent axis the walk surfaced

the `cross` column forced a question no free-list asked: **which seat on this box holds the
AWS badge?** on EC2 the answer is "every uid, via IMDS". here it is "whoever can read the cert
key". so a hidden axis sits under `badge` — the **badge source**:

| source | per-uid boundary? | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| `credential_process` over a 0600 cert + key in the camper's home | ✔ that uid, plus root and any sudo seat | 🟢 the design |
| `serve` — an IMDS-shaped endpoint on 127.0.0.1 | ✋ every uid on the box | **forbidden** — it rebuilds the EC2 hole on the laptop that holds the human's rack |
| `update` — writes `~/.aws/credentials` on a loop | ✔ if 0600, but a daemon + a second writer | itemized, rejected |
| an sso token or a copied static key | ✋ needs a human, or never expires | forbidden by the wish |

⇒ the source is not a free variable; it is fixed to `credential_process`, and the other three
are rejection invariants. that is a domain discovery the grid made, not a preference.
