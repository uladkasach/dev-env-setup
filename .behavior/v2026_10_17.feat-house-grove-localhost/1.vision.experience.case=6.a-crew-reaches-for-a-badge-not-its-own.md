# case=6 — a crew reaches for a badge not its own, and is refused

covers inventory rows 25, 26, 27. feel: 🗡️ sharp throughout — every step is an attempt the
behavior must refuse. care: 🔴 critipath — this boundary is the reason the wish says a house
grove is STRONGER than an EC2 one; if it leaks, that claim is false and the laptop that holds
the human's rack and sso tokens is exposed to every crew.

## .narrative

two house groves share the laptop: `house-1` runs an ahbode crew, `house-2` an ehmpathy crew.
a crew process on `house-2` goes astray and reaches for whatever badge it can find.

it tries `house-1-camper`'s cert: permission denied — the home is 0700. it tries the human's
home for `~/.aws/sso/cache`: permission denied — the human's home is not a grove's to read,
and the mint verifies that it is not group- or world-readable before it creates a seat beside
it. it scans 127.0.0.1 for an IMDS-shaped responder, the way a camper on grove-1 would curl
169.254.169.254: there is none, because the badge source is `credential_process`, which holds
the session in the caller's own memory and opens no port.

later a well-meant edit swaps the profile to `aws_signing_helper serve` "to cut sts calls". the
next converge of `house-2` fails ✋: the verify reads the ambient block, sees a serve endpoint,
and refuses — *"a loopback credential endpoint answers every uid on this box; that is the IMDS
hole this grove exists without"* — and names the line to restore.
