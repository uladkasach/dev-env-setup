# 🌙 dream: play.run --all names the play in flight

- kind: feat
- owner: dev-env-setup
- caught 2026-11-07

## .the cue that fired

the full suite on this tree, run `2026-11-07T17-22-07Z`, ~50 plays under `--within 900`: between
two verdict lines the terminal sat silent for minutes, and only a read of the log on disk said
which play held the run. `rule.require.status-feedback`: a wait longer than a moment shows
progress.

## .the gap, precisely

`--all` prints a play's line only when its verdict lands. while a play runs, the output names
neither the play nor how long it has run. a human cannot tell a slow play from a hung runner
without a second shell.

## .the shape of the fix

before each play, write `      ⏳ <slug> — in flight, bound <N>s` to STDERR only, so the verdict
tree on stdout and in `<stamp>.stdout.log` stays exactly as the reader's contract reads it (F2:
the tally stays last). write it always, never gated on a tty
(`rule.forbid.tty-as-a-proxy-for-a-human`). the clamp
then asserts the stderr line per play and that the `.stdout.log` holds none.

## .why it is not done in this round

a new output channel on the runner, outside the wish (the receipt); it shares the clamp, so a
later pr can add it with its own case.
