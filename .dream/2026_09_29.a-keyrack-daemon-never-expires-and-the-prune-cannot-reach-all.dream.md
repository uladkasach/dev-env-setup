# 🌙 dream: a keyrack daemon never expires, and the `@all` prune cannot reach one

- kind   = reseed
- owner  = a driver — both defects are `ehmpathy/rhachet`'s, and both are in shipped code
- caught = 2026-09-29

## .the cue that fired

`hazard.idle-process-leak-crosses-the-swap-cliff` ends with:

> Upstream repair is tracked in `.dream/…`

⇒ **it was not tracked.** the pointer named a dream that no run of `globsafe` finds, so the
sentence asserted a record that did not exist. the brief held the whole diagnosis and the queue
held no entry at all, which is the shape `philosophy.pavement-saves-nature` names: a measurement
with no owner is a fact, and a fact does not fix a daemon.

## .the gap, mechanically — two defects, and they compose

the brief measured **585 → 641 idle keyrack daemons in one session** on this laptop, each holding
anonymous pages that survive to the zram cliff. two causes, both upstream:

| # | the defect | what it costs |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | a `hasEverHadKeys` guard gates auto-termination, so a daemon that never held a key never exits | every daemon a test run mints is immortal |
| 2 | the `@all` prune glob is pinned to the **caller's own** `homeHash` | the mop cannot reach the daemons a throwaway `HOME` minted |

🔴 **they compose into an unreachable leak.** defect 1 mints the immortal daemon; defect 2 is why
the one tool that could clear it does not see it. either alone is an annoyance; together they are
a slow crawl to a swap cliff that no local command can walk back.

⚠️ **and the minting is CORRECT behavior.** `genTempDir` (a `test-fns` export) sets a throwaway
`HOME` per `*.integration.test.ts`, which mints a fresh socket hash, which spawns a fresh daemon.
**571 of 585 were minted by test runs.** test isolation is right; that the daemons outlive the
suite is the defect.

⇒ verified in the same measurement: production `.sh` skills set no `HOME`, so a real commit
inherits the real `HOME` and reuses one daemon. **the leak is a property of the test path alone**,
which is exactly why it goes unnoticed until a machine thrashes.

## .the shape of the fix

1. **retire the `hasEverHadKeys` gate on the expiry path.** a daemon's idle timer is about
   IDLENESS, and whether it ever held a key says no part of that. a daemon with no keys is the
   cheapest one to terminate, never the one to spare

2. **de-pin the `@all` prune glob from the caller's `homeHash`.** `@all` is a claim about
   EVERY daemon this user owns; a glob scoped to one `homeHash` answers a narrower question under
   the wider word (`term=keyrack.gitroot`, the same axis defect in a second costume)

3. re-prove by a BITE in both directions: mint a daemon under a throwaway `HOME`, confirm `@all`
   now lists it, confirm it self-terminates on its idle timer

## .why it is not done in this round

**it is not this repo's code.** the daemon and its prune live in `ehmpathy/rhachet`, and a local
patch would put a second reader over another package's process model
(`rule.always.reuse-pavement-before-improvise`, the braided-trail shape).

🟡 the cheap half IS taken here: `keyrack.daemon.prune` exists as the mop, and the hazard brief
now points at this dream rather than at open air.

## .what this dream does NOT claim

it does not re-measure the two defects. **the diagnosis is inherited from

an owner; it adds no evidence of its own (`rule.require.trust-but-verify` — re-read the rhachet
source before you patch on this page alone).
