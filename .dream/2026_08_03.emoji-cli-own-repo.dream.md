dream =

eject the emoji cli into its own `ehmpathy` repo.

---

## why

it is not dev-env-setup shaped. dev-env-setup configures *this* machine;
the emoji cli is a **tool** with a data contract, a rank contract, a test
suite, and an install story that would serve anyone on zsh.

three concrete pulls:

- **it has no local dependency.** the index comes from CLDR + unicode.org,
  the widget is pure zsh + awk, the picker is fzf. none of it needs vlad's
  machine.
- **it already earns a test suite.** 20 cases across three rebound keys.
  that wants CI, which dev-env-setup does not run for shell config.
- **the gap is real for others.** kitty's picker is names-only and its
  emoji tab is hardcoded to 80 chars. `uni` prints to stdout but does not
  insert inline. the "type it, tab it, it lands at the cursor" gesture
  appears to have no incumbent.

dev-env-setup would keep only `install_emoji`, which fetches the released
artifact rather than carries the source.

## the hard-won parts to carry over

these cost real time to find, and are the reason a fresh reimplementation
would come out worse:

1. **the FE0F trap.** CLDR keys `❤` as `2764`; emoji-test.txt lists the
   fully-qualified `2764 FE0F`. a byte-match intersect silently drops 655
   emoji — ❤️ and ⚠️ among them. match on the stripped form, emit the
   qualified form. both are canaries in the verify set.
2. **`match`, not `contains`, on the trigger.** measured over 20,499 real
   commands: 66 contain a colon, **0** begin a word with one. the
   word-start anchor is the entire safety argument.
3. **one awk, not `awk | sort | cut`.** 48.2 ms → 15.7 ms. it runs per
   keystroke.
4. **`trap 'exit 0' PIPE`, never `trap '' PIPE`.** ignore is not handle;
   ignore makes printf report EPIPE, which is the error it meant to remove.
5. **chain TAB to the prior bind, captured at load** — never to a guessed
   `expand-or-complete`.

## open

- name? `emoji-zsh`, `zmoji`, `:emoji:`
- scope: zsh only, or add a bash readline bind and an nvim source? the
  nvim question is already open in the vision — it may answer itself here,
  since a standalone repo makes multi-frontend natural.
- does it ship the index, or build on install? built is ~96 KB, so either
  works; built-on-install stays current as unicode ships yearly.

## when

after `.behavior/v2026_08_02.emoji-cli` reaches verification. eject what
works, with its tests — not what might.
