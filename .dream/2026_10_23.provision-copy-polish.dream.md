## .the gap, precisely

- **emphasis caps**, against `rule.forbid.shouts` and `rule.prefer.lowercase`. `BOX-WIDE` is at
  `src/bundle.upgrade.sh:363`, and `INTEGRITY CHECK FAILED` is in `src/grove.web.sh`. the same
  register shows up as `STALE`, `WRONG`, `EVERY`, `REMOVED`, `PRIOR`, `OLD`, `SILENT`, `AT SPAWN`
  and `EMITS` across `2.8.tmux`.
- **the `(s)` plural**, where the count is known: `1 live server(s)`, `all 1 live pane(s)`,
  `2 socket(s)`.
- **`read why:` on a row that changes state** or is no command at all: `sysctl --system`,
  `sudo loginctl enable-linger`, `sudo apt-get install …`, `tmux -L … source-file`, a bare url.
  rows that change state belong under `fix:`.
- **layout drift within one family**: the `⚠️` row sits at 5 spaces in configure and 6 in verify.
  `cause:` comes before the `⇒` rows in C2/C4 and after them in G. the `A`/`M` integrity block
  runs 14 lines, names its causes twice in two vocabularies, and says "(see _.sh)" with no path.
- **mask words** in the play snaps: `<varies by box>`, `<the pane cgroup, …>`, and
  `<the reproduction's verdict, …>` are three wordings for one idea.

## .the shape of the fix

1. one pass per register: lowercase emphasis, a `_plural <n> <word>` helper in the runtime,
   `read why:` → `fix:` for any row that writes state
2. one shared indent for `⚠️` and `cause:` (6 spaces, under the header's text). `cause:` goes
   directly under the header, ahead of every `⇒`
3. the integrity block: one cause list in one vocabulary, and a real path in place of "(see _.sh)"
4. one mask token in the plays, `<varies>`, defined in `.snap.operations.sh`
5. resnap every touched play and read each diff. every change should be copy only, never a
   change in the exit code or the claim

## .why it is not done in this round

safe, not clean. it touches `src/bundle.upgrade.sh` and `src/grove.web.sh`, which every bundle
calls, plus copy in a dozen bundles and plays the route did not otherwise open, and each touched
play needs its own resnap review. the 5.3 stone grades whether what was built is proven, and a
register sweep across the tree would bury that diff.
