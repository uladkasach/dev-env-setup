## .why it is not done in this round

none of the seven is a step a grove needs a human for: each is a path the provision does not
take on a healthy box, or a message that already names its cause one hop later. they want
their own fixtures (14 needs a failed clone over the wire; 13 needs a real sudo with no ticket),
and this round spent its budget on the five paths that could strand a first boot.
