# 🌙 dream: the verification guard binds test lanes to `*.test.ts`, which a play-tested repo never holds

kind: reseed · owner: the behavior route template (ehmpathy/rhachet-roles-bhuild) · caught 2026-11-21

## .the cue that fired

`5.3.verification` lane `mech-test-scope-purity` returned `✋ ConstraintError: combined scope
resolves to zero files` — `--paths-with '{src,blackbox}/**/*.test.ts'` ∩ `since-main` (89 files) = 0.
three more lanes (`behavior-experience-coverage`, `mech-given-when-then`, `mech-test-intent`) bind
`**/*.test.ts` plus `**/*.snap`, so in this repo they read the snaps alone and never a test.

## .the gap, precisely

this repo proves every bundle with a play (`.play/permanent/*.play.sh`); it holds no `.test.ts` and
no jest. the template's test-lane binds assume a jest repo, so on a play-tested repo one lane is
dark every round and three see half their subject — and the guard says so in no line.

## .the shape of the fix

- in the template: derive the test glob from the repo (a declared `tests:` glob, or a probe for
  `*.test.ts` vs `.play/**/*.play.sh`), or add the play glob beside the jest one
- here: once the mutate grant stands, rebind the four lanes in `5.3.verification.guard` to
  `.play/permanent/*.play.sh` plus `**/*.snap`

## .why it is not done in this round

the guard edit needs the route's mutate grant (a human's), and the template lives in another repo —
a reseed, never an adoption here.
