## .the shape of the fix

two plays under `.play/permanent/`:

- `prove.bundles.plan-apply-apply.play.sh` — a declared `WHAT_ALL` list; per slug: plan, apply,
  apply into `~/.grove.proof/<slug>.{1.plan,2.apply,3.reapply}.txt`, then a diff into `.4.drift.txt`.
  verdicts: plan mutated (an upsert line without `— would run (plan)`, never a word search),
  not idempotent, prompt opened, apply non-zero both runs (judged, never auto-failed). the verdict
  table already lives in `howto.prove-bundles-plan-apply-apply`
- `prove.tree.fixed-point.play.sh` — full apply twice, full plan; every claim left must be a
  credential claim

then restore the `--play` lines in the three briefs and the howto.

## .why it is not done in this round

safe, not clean: a play is proven only by a run on a grove, and no grove is reachable from this
worktree today (the keyrack org allowlist). an unrun play shipped as the paved path would be a
clamp nobody saw bite. the doc repair is the safe half, and it landed.
