## .the gap, precisely

- the arms run the finder box-wide and read one pid out of it, so the verdict depends on every
  other process on a shared box; a foreign burn crowds the scratch pid out of the report
- the survivor check runs `kill -0` straight after `kill`, before the exec'd `sleep` is reaped, so
  it can report a process that is already on its way down

## .the shape of the fix

`src/machine/machine_resource_procs_find_spinner`, or a read of only their rows), and in cleanup
`wait` on each scratch pid before the `kill -0` check.
