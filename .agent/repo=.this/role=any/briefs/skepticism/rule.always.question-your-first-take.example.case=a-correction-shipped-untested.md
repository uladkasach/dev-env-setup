## .the take that shipped

the human said a laptop should never hold a camp key. the next take, sent at once:

*the defect is `2.2.git` — it installs the camp-key helper on every grove kind with no tier gate.*

it was retracted one reply later.

## .the counter-take in view

the helper is installed on every kind on purpose, and the briefs say so: it is inert on an ssh
remote, and a laptop's remotes are ssh. a helper that does no harm where it sits is not the
cause of a push that took the https path.

## .the read that would have broken it

`define.github-auth-two-paths`, the table of which piece runs on which kind — the helper and
its config are marked `any`, the https rewrite alone is `cloud`.

## .the cost

the correction was treated as the next answer to deliver, under the same unchecked stop
condition, so the human had to correct the correction.
