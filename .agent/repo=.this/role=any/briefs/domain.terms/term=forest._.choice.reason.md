# domain.term.choice.reason: forest

## .etymology
the widest level of the metaphor: a **forest** is every grove at once. where a grove is one
machine and a tree is one branch workspace, the forest is the whole fleet seen as one — the
answer to "where are all my trees, across all machines?"

chosen over:
- `fleet` / `cluster` — mechanical, and severed from the tree/grove metaphor
- `inventory` — a list, not a place; forest is the whole the groves compose

## .disputes
none yet.

## .evidence
- decomposition: the where-does-work-happen axis has three levels — tree (branch) ⊂ grove
  (machine) ⊂ forest (fleet); forest is the widest cell, all groves united
- narrative: `git tree status` with no grove renders the forest view — groves listed, each
  with its trees, so a developer sees the whole fleet at a glance
- dreams: `.dream/2026_06_17.git-forest.dream.md` names the forest view + `~/.git.forest/`
  registry of grove membership
