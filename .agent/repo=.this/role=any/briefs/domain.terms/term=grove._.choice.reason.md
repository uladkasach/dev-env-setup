# domain.term.choice.reason: grove

## .etymology
in the forest metaphor a **grove** is a stand of trees rooted in one plot of ground — so a
grove is one machine, and the trees (branch workspaces) it holds are rooted there. wake a
grove and its trees are reachable; hibernate it and they sleep together.

chosen over:
- `machine` / `box` / `node` — flat, mechanical, and blind to the forest metaphor that
  binds tree ⊂ grove ⊂ forest into one vocabulary
- `host` — reserved for ductwork's transport vantage (`user@host:name`); grove is the
  git-forest vantage on the same physical machine. two layers, one machine, kept distinct

## .disputes
none yet.

## .evidence
- decomposition: the where-does-work-happen axis has three levels — tree (branch) ⊂ grove
  (machine) ⊂ forest (fleet); grove is the middle cell, the machine that holds trees
- narrative: a developer wakes a cloud grove, then opens trees on it; laptop sleeps, the
  grove hibernates, its trees preserved; next day the grove wakes and the trees resume
- dreams: `.dream/2026_06_17.git-forest.dream.md` (git.grove.list/wake/add/del) and
  `.dream/2026_06_14.cloud-agent-host.dream.md` (groves as hibernatable ec2 hosts)
- parallel: ductwork's `user@host` is the same machine named at the transport layer
