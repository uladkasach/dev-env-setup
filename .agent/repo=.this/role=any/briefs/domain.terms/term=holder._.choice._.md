term.synonyms.forbidden:

- duplicate

## .what

one of the N places a single FACT is written down. two holders of one fact are free to
drift, and the drift has no signal — which is measurement **m.9**, the shape this repo
retires most often.

a holder is not merely a second copy. it is a second copy **that a reader trusts
independently**, so a defect in one is invisible to every reader of the others.

## .the test

> **how many places state this fact, and does a reader of one ever consult another?**

- one holder → the fact is safe; a change reaches every reader
- two, and each is read alone → they will disagree, and the cheaper one stays wrong
