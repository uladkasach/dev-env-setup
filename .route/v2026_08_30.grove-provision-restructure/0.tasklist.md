### 🛑 the cut this section must make — and it is not "delete the history"

`rule.forbid.chronological-accretion` and `rule.require.timeless-comments` say: state current
truth, let git hold the how. `gotcha.my-own-note-became-my-evidence` argues the other way — a
claim silently corrected reads as though it was always right, and the next author re-derives it.
⇒ so the cut is by what the record changes, never by AGE:

| the record | verdict | why |
|---|---|---|
| a **trap** a future author would re-walk into (`$HOME is not a sandbox`, `a tty is not a human`, `--reply supersedes --bare`) | **keep** — a rule, not a chronicle | the reader needs it BEFORE the next line |
| a **measurement** that is the sole evidence for a live claim (m.1-m.12) | **keep**, as a claim with its evidence | the claim is unproven without it |
| a **chronicle of the edit** (*"this line read X until 2026-08-13"*) | **wick up** | git holds the old sentence |
| a **self-reproach** with no rule extracted | **wick up** | it costs tokens every boot and teaches no move |
